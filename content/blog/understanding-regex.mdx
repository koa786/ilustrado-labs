Regular expressions (regex) are pattern-matching strings used to search, extract, and replace text patterns across programming languages.

## Essential Regex Patterns

<Table
  headers={["Pattern", "Description", "Example Match"]}
  rows={[
    ["\\d+", "One or more digits", "1234"],
    ["[a-zA-Z]+", "One or more letters", "Hello"],
    ["^\\w+@[a-zA-Z_]+?\\.[a-zA-Z]{2,3}$", "Basic Email Match", "user@example.com"],
    ["https?://[^\\s]+", "URL Pattern", "https://ilustradolabs.com"]
  ]}
/>

### Flags to Remember

* `g` - Global match (find all occurrences)
* `i` - Case-insensitive matching
* `m` - Multi-line matching
